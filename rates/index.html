How these numbers are made

Each run asks the question fresh and saves the full answer. A script checks every saved answer for up to three things, as far as each engine exposes them. Did a page from the target site appear in the search results the engine read, was the site cited as a source, and was the business named in the answer text, with links removed. Those counts become the rows above.

The ranges are exact 95% intervals (Clopper-Pearson). A run where a step could not be read is left out of that row and noted on it. Each range describes one engine, place and time window, never a person's own ChatGPT session. A name in the answer is counted as a mention. Whether the answer recommends the business takes a human read, and the lab log entries do that.
