EXP-025Open

Does a published Business Agent on EVOIX's ChatGPT profile answer customers accurately, and does publishing it change how ChatGPT presents the business?

The EVOIX profile in ChatGPT's Business Hub. Tested in preview November 15, published November 16 (UTC).

Predicted
At least 18 of 20 locked test questions answered consistently with the business information entered (medium). No answer promises a result (medium high). At least one answer uses a fact from the website that is not in the profile (medium). Question 17 is the likeliest to fail (low medium that it passes).
Result
20 of 20 consistent, with no guarantees, no invented prices and no invented client results. No answer used a website fact outside the profile, so that prediction missed. Question 17 passed cleanly on the first run, so the call that it would fail was wrong, though a later run missed it, saying the agent had no phone or email on file when both were entered. Two of the twenty answers contained an em dash.
Publishing
OpenAI's review refused 11 of 18 publish attempts. In the 10 refusals where the reply was recorded, it was one error code and no reason. Removing elements one at a time pointed to two of them, a heading that read "Honesty rules" and a paragraph telling the agent not to rate competitors. Each finding rests on a single pair of attempts, and the review may not be consistent. The agent went live without them.
Status
Part two, whether publishing changes how ChatGPT presents EVOIX, reads November 17, 23 and 30. The platform's own dashboard numbers are confidential under OpenAI's terms and are not published here.
